# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Tech Leads

### Role Summary
Tech Leads provide technical direction for the team and help translate product goals into implementable architecture and delivery plans.

### Responsibilities
- Guide technical design, architecture decisions, and implementation standards
- Break down complex initiatives into deliverable technical milestones
- Support estimation, dependency mapping, and technical risk management
- Mentor developers through design reviews and pairing

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to evaluate technical trade-offs for roadmap items
- Collaborate with Project Managers on sequencing, dependencies, and delivery risk
- Work closely with Developers to align implementation with architecture decisions
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on test strategy for critical technical changes

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture decision records
- Sprint planning inputs on complexity and sequencing
- Ongoing code review and implementation guidance

---

## Release Managers

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate release readiness and execution to ensure changes move to production safely and predictably.

### Responsibilities
- Define release scope, schedule, and go/no-go readiness criteria
- Track release blockers, dependencies, and rollback plans
- Coordinate change windows, release notes, and stakeholder notifications
- Verify operational readiness with engineering and QA/Testing

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Align with Project Managers on milestones and release timing risks
- Coordinate with Tech Leads and Developers on technical readiness and rollback options
- Partner with QA/Testing to confirm validation status before release
- Communicate release status and impacts to Stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Release calendars and readiness checklists
- Go/no-go meetings and launch coordination channels
- Post-release summaries including incidents and follow-up actions

---

## UX/UI Designers

### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers shape user experience and interface design so teams can deliver usable, accessible, and coherent product outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define user flows, wireframes, and visual design guidance
- Validate usability through prototypes, feedback, and iterative refinement
- Ensure accessibility and consistency across product experiences
- Support clear design handoff to engineering teams

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with Product Managers to align design direction with user needs and priorities
- Work with Developers and Tech Leads to ensure feasible implementation details
- Partner with QA/Testing on usability and accessibility acceptance criteria
- Share rationale and design decisions with Stakeholders during reviews

### Typical Communication
- Design critiques and prototype walkthroughs
- Handoff documentation with annotated flows and component usage
- Feedback sessions with product and engineering partners

---

## Security Champions

### Role Summary
Security Champions embed security practices into day-to-day delivery by helping teams identify and mitigate risks early.

### Responsibilities
- Promote secure design and coding practices within the team
- Review threat scenarios and prioritize mitigation actions
- Support security-focused checks in development and release workflows
- Escalate high-risk findings and track remediation with owners

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Support Developers and Tech Leads with secure implementation guidance
- Partner with Project Managers on risk tracking and escalation paths
- Align with Release Managers on security gates and exception handling
- Provide security risk visibility to Product Managers and Stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Security checklists in planning and definition-of-done discussions
- Risk reviews tied to significant feature or architecture changes
- Remediation tracking updates for prioritized security issues

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts clarify business needs and process impacts, helping teams convert stakeholder goals into actionable requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document requirements, constraints, and assumptions
- Map current and future-state workflows to identify gaps
- Define acceptance-focused requirement details with clear business context
- Support impact analysis for scope changes and dependencies

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partner with Product Managers to refine backlog items and success criteria
- Coordinate with Project Managers on scope clarity and dependency alignment
- Collaborate with Developers and QA/Testing to reduce ambiguity in requirements
- Validate business expectations and outcomes with Stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Requirement workshops and process-mapping sessions
- Structured requirement notes linked to backlog items
- Scope clarification updates during planning and delivery
